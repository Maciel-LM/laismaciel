# 🧬 Hi, I'm Laís Maciel

### Bioinformatician | Computational Biology | NGS, RNA-seq & Transcriptomics | Genomics & Multi-omics | Reproducible Pipelines | Python, R, Bash, Nextflow & Docker

I am a **Biomedical Scientist and Bioinformatician** working at the intersection of
**biomedicine, genomics, transcriptomics, and computational biology**.

My background in biomedical research and laboratory environments allows me to
connect biological questions with computational approaches, from raw sequencing
data to reproducible analyses and biological interpretation.

Currently pursuing a **PhD in Genetics and Molecular Biology**, I am particularly
interested in **NGS, RNA-seq, differential expression, genomic variation, and
computational approaches applied to biomedical research**.
